# Synthetic samples

Both files are generated by the C++ tool `generate_samples`; no ANSYS export or
third-party CAD data is used. Generated sample data is dedicated under CC0 1.0.

- triangle.stl: one synthetic ASCII triangle. STL itself has no units; assume
  metres for this example only.
- thermal_rod.csv: synthetic point samples of the analytic rod temperature, not
  measured temperatures. Coordinates are metres, temperatures degrees Celsius.
  Schema: node_id,x_m,y_m,z_m,temperature_C. IDs are unique integers.

Regenerate: `./build/generate_samples data`.
